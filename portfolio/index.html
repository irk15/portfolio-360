About

I design it, animate it, then write the code that ships it.

I'm a multimedia designer in Toronto with six years in the field. By day I design for Leon's Furniture. On my own time I run a one-person studio for AI video ads, brand identity, websites and social content.

I build 3D worlds and small tools because I like seeing an idea run, and I want every project to feel designed all the way down to the code.
