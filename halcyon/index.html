Introducing Halcyon One

Silence, shaped.

Wireless earbuds carved like river stones. Quiet by design.

From $249 · Ships spring
SCROLL
01 · THE CASE

Opens like a stone splitting in two.

Two buds, held by magnets, rise to meet you.

A single hinge, milled to open with one thumb and close with a quiet, weighted click.

02 · DESIGN

Engineered in layers.

Six parts, 4.8 grams, tuned together so nothing rattles, hisses or presses where it shouldn't.

Exploded view · six parts

Then sealed into one smooth pebble.

No stems, no seams you can feel. It sits in the curve of your ear and disappears.
