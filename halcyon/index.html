FIG. 03 · EXPLODED VIEW
02 · Design

Engineered in layers.

  1. Acoustic meshLaser-cut hex grille over a vented chamber.
  2. Pebble shellSoft-touch composite, 4.8 g per bud.
  3. H1 audio coreRuns noise cancelling 48,000 times a second.
  4. Silicon-carbon cellNine hours on a single charge.
  5. 11 mm driverCopper-dome diaphragm for clean, deep bass.
  6. Memory-foam tipSeals gently. Four sizes in the box.
